I'm a cybersecurity professional with over four years of experience in Security Operations and currently work as an Information Security Tech Sr. Analyst. Throughout my career, I've worked in enterprise environments, analyzing security events, investigating suspicious activity, and helping organizations distinguish legitimate threats from benign activity through evidence-driven investigations.

In my current role, I use a SIEM platform to analyze security telemetry across enterprise environments. When security events require further analysis, I analyze assigned cases by correlating relevant log sources, reviewing user and host activity, and constructing event timelines to determine whether the observed behavior represents a false positive or a potential security incident. Beyond case investigations, I contribute to operational efficiency by mentoring newly onboarded analysts, maintaining internal investigation documentation and playbooks, and identifying recurring false positives. When tuning opportunities are identified, I gather supporting evidence and submit recommendations through the review process, collaborating with the teams responsible for implementing detection improvements.

I created this website as a place to share technical write-ups, hands-on projects, and practical notes while building a portfolio that reflects my work and interests. Whether I'm documenting a lab, analyzing an attack technique, or exploring a security concept, my goal is to produce content that is technically accurate, practical, and useful to security professionals and fellow learners.

Outside of work, I continue developing my skills through hands-on training and personal projects. My current focus is completing the Hack The Box Certified Penetration Testing Specialist (CPTS) learning path, with plans to pursue the Offensive Security Certified Professional (OSCP) afterward.
